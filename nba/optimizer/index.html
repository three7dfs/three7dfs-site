Late swap is a Premium feature.

  1. On DraftKings: My Contests → Upcoming → Edit Entries (NBA Classic) → Download. That's DKEntries.csv.
  2. Choose it here and press Late swap. Players whose game has tipped stay; every other spot gets our best projection (with your edits and uploads).
  3. Download the new file and upload it on the same DraftKings page. Your file never leaves this browser.
